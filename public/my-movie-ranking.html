The Best Movies According to Download Genius

My top 3 movies of all-time

The Matrix

A computer hacker discovers that his life is nothing more than an elaborate simulation run by an evil AI.

The Wizard of Oz

Young Dorothy Gale and her dog Toto are swept away by a tornado from their Kansas farm to the magical Land of Oz and embark on a quest with three new friends to see the Wizard, who can return her to her home and fulfill the others' wishes.

The Philadelphia Story

After a rich woman's ex-husband and a tabloid-type reporter turn up just before her planned remarriage, she begins to learn the truth about herself.
